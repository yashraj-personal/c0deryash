Privacy policy

This is a small creative studio by YASH RAJ. We keep data collection intentionally limited and use it only to keep the experience working and improve the experiments.

What we collect

The site may record anonymous page views and interactions such as the tool used, route visited, and basic control labels. We do not ask for your name, email, precise location, or payment information.

How it is used

Activity data is used to understand which creative tools are useful, find errors, and improve performance. It is stored in Supabase with row-level security and is not publicly readable.

Your choices

You can leave the site at any time. Browser session identifiers are temporary and are not used to identify you personally. Generated posters and videos are downloaded directly to your device and are not uploaded by this site.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact YASH RAJ through Instagram.
